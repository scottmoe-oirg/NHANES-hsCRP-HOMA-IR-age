\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]
\footnotesize
\item Values in the age-group columns are HOMA-IR coefficients with 95\% confidence intervals where available.
For models with $\ln$(hs-CRP) as the outcome, coefficients are on the log outcome scale; raw-outcome coefficients are in hs-CRP units.
All analyses used the NHANES complex survey design with duration-adjusted fasting-subsample weights,
masked variance strata, and masked variance units.
\item[a] To isolate the adiposity specification itself, the BMI analysis was restricted to participants in the primary analytic sample who also had nonmissing BMI.
\item[b] Participants with diagnosed diabetes remained excluded; only the HbA1c eligibility threshold was removed.
\item[c] Each refit deleted one unique masked stratum--PSU combination from the locked primary model.
These sensitivity analyses were performed to evaluate robustness and were not treated as additional primary hypothesis tests.
